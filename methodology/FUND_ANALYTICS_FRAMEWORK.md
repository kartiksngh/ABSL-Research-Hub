# Fund & AMC Analytics Framework

## Objective
For every fund, move from **data -> portfolio facts -> attribution -> diagnosis -> forward evidence -> action**.

Every material active-equity scheme should receive a genuine deep dive. Do not reduce most funds to one-slide scorecards.

## Mandatory fund deep dive
Include:
- performance and peer standing
- rolling returns and consistency
- drawdown, capture and risk
- current holdings fingerprint
- cap, sector, industry and style positioning
- benchmark and peer-relative active weights
- unique positions and benchmark omissions
- 1M/3M/6M/12M portfolio changes
- quantity-vs-price decomposition
- stock contribution and sector/theme attribution
- earnings-revision overlay
- ownership, flow, crowding and liquidity
- theme health
- blind spots
- style-preserving actions
- FM decision dashboard

## Decision layer
Separate:
- What is working
- What is not working
- Blind spots
- KEEP / ADD / REDUCE / EXIT-REVIEW / MONITOR / CONDITIONAL

Recommendations must preserve the fund's inherent style.

## AMC/CIO synthesis
Aggregate bottom-up across schemes:
- performance map
- AUM and product architecture
- stock/sector/theme/factor matrices
- overlap
- common house convictions
- accidental duplication
- AMC-level active exposures
- revision health
- ABSL vs industry ownership/flow differentiation
- liquidity and simultaneous-exit risk
- style drift
- product differentiation
- strongest/weakest portfolio decisions
- resilience recommendations
- 30/60/90-day CIO action matrix

## Presentation standard
Prefer charts, heatmaps, percentile plots, contribution bars, waterfalls, overlap matrices and decision dashboards over prose.
